projects & experience

The fastest way to understand a system is to build it. These are the pipelines, simulations, and measurement tools I've built along the way.

selected projects
5G NSA Throughput & RF Performance Diagnostics
AERPAW · NC State
Integrated throughput, GPS, LTE/NR KPIs, cell identifiers, EN-DC logs, QNWCFG records, and UAV telemetry into a common post-processing workflow. Measured 210 Mbps median throughput near 27 LTE cell-ID transitions versus 315 Mbps for control periods, and compared EN-DC and QNWCFG modem records to pinpoint transition-related NR RF reporting differences.
UAV LTE Raw-I/Q Signal-Quality Analysis
AERPAW · NC State
Built a Python LTE pipeline with synchronization, CFO correction, cell search, PCI filtering, CRS extraction, PSD features, and GPS geometry across 4,952 valid captures near 3.51 GHz. Adding spectrum-derived features cut hard-holdout CRS-quality MAE by 63–87% over geometry-only baselines. Route-holdout MAE of 7.63 dB and leave-altitude-out MAE of 8.69 dB exposed the generalization gap hidden by random splits.
End-to-End OFDM Link Simulation
MATLAB
Implemented a full OFDM link with multipath fading, timing synchronization, channel estimation, CFO correction, ZF/MMSE equalization, CPE correction, and BER/EVM analysis. Reduced 64-QAM BER at 30 dB from 0.293 to 0.000142 after integrating timing, CFO, and common-phase-error corrections.
Altitude-Dependent Spectrum & Propagation Modeling
AERPAW · NC State
Modeled received-power and spectral variation across UAV and helikite altitudes using path loss, power, entropy, sparsity, and LoS/NLoS indicators. Identified a sharp urban LoS transition near 50 m and a gradual rural one. Calibrated inter-year downlink and CBRS models using only two target-year altitude bins, achieving below 4 dB RMSE in stable transfer cases.
Terrain-Aware Propagation Simulation
CableLabs
Integrated Longley–Rice/ITM into a MATLAB system simulator with configurable frequency, antenna, terrain-profile, and propagation parameters. Compared terrain-resolution and offline-processing tradeoffs across SRTM, GMTED2010, DTED, USGS, and NTIA elevation sources.
